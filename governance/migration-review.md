# Migration and schema-drift control

The register contains one already-applied P0 security-control migration, its inventory-only Supabase CLI representation, and four ordered P1 product migrations persistently applied to Rosuno Staging. P1-001, the original P1-002 migration, P1-003, and the P1-002 sequence-5 correction have completed reviewed traceability records. The original P1-002 SQL and pre-review evidence remain immutable historical records; current lifecycle evidence records their later protected approval, application, validation-evidence closure, defect discovery, forward-only correction, and final closure. The P0 inventory representation is sourced from exactly one read-only Staging history statement, does not authorize replay, and does not mutate remote history or schema.

Every migration record must have a unique ordered identifier, an accountable owner, reviewer state, authority, decision, work-item and artifact references, a dependency list, a rollback plan, validation evidence, and an accepted drift result or exact documented tooling exception with compensating catalog controls. A proposed, unapplied migration may temporarily have no release reference while protected closure remains pending; a reviewed or persistently applied migration may not remain pending and requires bidirectional release traceability. Known development or staging targets require non-production validation. Migrations must be reviewed and applied in order. Every discovered migration artifact must have exactly one register entry, and every register entry must resolve to one artifact. Missing predecessors, duplicate sequence numbers, unknown references, unregistered files, unsupported environment claims, or unverified application-history claims fail the gate.

The baseline under `governance/schema-drift/baseline.json` is the deterministic current foundation baseline for Rosuno Staging. Its immutable digest is recomputed from the Staging project identity, the exact five reviewed migration artifacts and SHA-256 digests, accepted P1-003 and P1-002 correction evidence, and the corrected V2 catalog fingerprint. It does not claim that the unavailable Supabase CLI shadow comparison ran; the accepted evidence retains that tooling limitation and the compensating direct-catalog validation. Empty product-schema state, missing or inconsistent Staging evidence, identity or digest mismatch, and contradictory migration inventory fail the gate. Any future baseline transition requires independently approved scope.
